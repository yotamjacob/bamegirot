Home contents and estate appraisal in Israel, from Tel Aviv to Haifa

Lior Buchstab · Bamegirot (במגירות) · Tel Aviv to Haifa

In short: Bamegirot is a home contents and estate appraisal service in Israel, run by Lior Buchstab. She visits the apartment, tells the family what is worth something and what is not, recommends where and how to sell each item (online, at an open-house sale or at auction), and guides the sale. Service area: Tel Aviv to Haifa.

Who it is for

Families who need to empty a parent's apartment, heirs dividing an estate, and anyone who suspects that a painting, a silver set or a piece of furniture may be worth more than a clearance company would assume. The right order is simple: first find out what is there, then decide what to do with it. Clearance comes last, not first.

What Lior appraises

  • Paintings and framed art
  • Silver and silver-plated metalware
  • Israeli ceramics, including Naaman and Lapid
  • Vintage and antique furniture
  • Complete home contents and estates

How it works

  1. Tell her what you have. A call or a few WhatsApp photos: a whole home, an inheritance or a collection.
  2. An honest appraisal. What is worth something, what is worth little, and the right way to handle the contents. No obligation.
  3. A guided sale. Each item goes to the channel that suits it: online, an open-house sale or an auction house. The family decides at every step.

What Bamegirot is not

Not a certified appraiser for court or insurance purposes, not a contents dealer who buys the apartment's contents for cash, and not a removal company. When one of those is what a family needs, Lior says so.

Service area

Tel Aviv, Ramat Hasharon, Herzliya, Ra'anana, Kfar Saba, Hod Hasharon, Netanya, Hadera, Zichron Yaakov, Haifa. The service is provided in Hebrew.

Frequently asked questions

Does Bamegirot buy the contents of the apartment?

No. Lior appraises the items, recommends where and how to sell each one, and guides the sale. Items are sold to buyers on the open market, so she has no interest in a low price.

Is Lior a certified appraiser for court or insurance?

No. For a signed valuation required by a court, an insurer or a formal estate division, a certified appraiser (shamai musmach) is the right professional. Bamegirot helps families who want to know what is worth something and sell it well.

Which areas does Bamegirot serve?
